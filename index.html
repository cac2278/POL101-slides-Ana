CALL IT OUT · FIND BETTER COVERAGE

T
TRACE TO THE ORIGINAL Where did THAT come from?
“a study found…” → study “the governor said…” → speech “the law requires…” → law

Follow the quote, statistic, image, study, or policy reference back to its original context.

Government-domain clues: U.S. .gov · UK .gov.uk · Kenya .go.ke · Ghana .gov.gh
Clue, not proof: verify the government body.

✓ This may lead you to the actual policy.
